“Equivalence over Similarity: Hidden-Layer Verification as a Filter and a Metric for Fortran-to-C++ Translation,” led by R. Imai (Long Paper), received the Outstanding Paper Award. A second paper, “VeCaRP: A Cache Replacement Policy Exploiting Vector Code Context,” led by S. Nomura, was also accepted at CANDAR 2026 (Regular Paper). Both papers will be presented in Ishigaki, Japan.
